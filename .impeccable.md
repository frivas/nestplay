## Design Context

### Users
One person on the Living Room Apple TV (10-foot Siri Remote), browsing a private local library of movies and downloaded videos after a LAN health check.

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, warm, nest-like. Midnight teal stage, gold nest accent, cream type — the Nest egg mark. Calm living-room confidence, not a dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
tvOS film shelves: landscape 16:9 posters, native focus, uncluttered. Dark teal (not pure black), cream titles, gold only on focus. Anti-references: filename lists, YouTube chips/Shorts, cyan-on-dark “AI” glow, TMDB/Netflix clones, desktop grids.

### Design Principles
1. The local library is the opening surface; never call it Home.
2. Posters are the catalog; chrome and debug copy stay out of the way.
3. Artwork comes from the file on the Mac, never a path on the wire, never TMDB on this increment.
4. Focus must work on Apple TV HD with the Siri Remote.
5. Empty and failed artwork still look designed, not broken.
