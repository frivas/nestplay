## Question

Is a one-time local YouTube session-cookie import acceptable for the PoC, with local re-import when reconnecting, or is an integrated browser sign-in mandatory before proceeding? No cookies should be pasted into conversation or sent to Apple TV. The documented adapter supports cookies; a seamless browser handoff and automatic renewal are not proven. User acceptance is required and has not been inferred from agreement to Mac-side sign-in.
